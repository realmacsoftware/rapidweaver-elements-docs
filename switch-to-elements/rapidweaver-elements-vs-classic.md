# Switching to Elements

If you're looking to move over from Classic? Start here. The video below will give you a good overview of how Classic and Elements differ. We'll walk you through each difference to help you get more comfortable building websites in Elements.

The switch might feel like a big step, but the improved performance, future-proof tools, and enhanced creative freedom make it well worth the journey. Plus, the supportive [Elements community](https://forums.realmacsoftware.com/) is here to help you every step of the way!
