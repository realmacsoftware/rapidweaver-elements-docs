# Elements Store

An Elements Cloud account is required to download and install products from the Elements Store.

#### Finding the Store

Over time the store will be integrated more tightly into the app, but for now, you can open the Elements Store by clicking on the "Store" icon in the Elements toolbar.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2025-11-13 at 10 .52.50@2x.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Store Benefits

The Elements Store brings a whole host of benefits, including:

* Access all previous purchases instantly by signing into your Elements Cloud account.
* Automatic download and installation of products.
* No need to manually back-up or track purchases.
* Instant in-app product Updates.
* All purchases are securely stored in your Elements Cloud account.
  * Ability to re-download purchased products even if the seller is no longer in business.
* Seamless product trials (Coming soon).

#### Product Refunds

We provide a straightforward 7-day refund policy for all store purchases. If you’d like a refund, [contact us directly](https://www.realmacsoftware.com/support/) and we’ll take care of it. We handle all refunds ourselves, so there’s no need to reach out to any third-party developers.

**Please note:** Once the refund has been processed the installed product will switch to back to Trial mode.

#### Distribute your Products on the Store

We have an [in-depth guide](https://docs.realmacsoftware.com/elements-docs/elements-language/store/getting-started) to help you get your products on the Elements Store. It covers everything you need to know about submitting and promoting your products to the growing number of passionate RapidWeaver Element users around the world.
