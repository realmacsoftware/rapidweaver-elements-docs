# Adding a Product

Before you submit your product, review the [Submission Requirements](../submission-requirements/) as it includes the current thumbnail and screenshot specs.

Open the Store window in Elements, then select **Dev Packs** under **Developer** in the sidebar.

From there, you can manage installed components and submit them to the Store.

Each product submission has two parts:

1. **Build** — the dev pack you upload for review.
2. **Info** — the listing details, pricing, and screenshots shown in the Store.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Make sure your pack defines valid [categories in info.json](https://docs.realmacsoftware.com/elements-docs/elements-language/element-pack/info-json). If not, your submission will fail.
{% endhint %}
