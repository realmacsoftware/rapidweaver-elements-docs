# Early Access

### Elements has officially reached 1.0

After years of hard-work, testing, and community feedback, Elements is now a fully released 1.0 product, ready for real-world projects. You can now choose to [try it for free](download-trial.md) with the new trial mode, or [purchase a license](https://elementsapp.io/) to unlock the full experience. Whether you’re building a personal site or launching client projects, Elements is ready when you are 🎉

#### 1.0 is just the beginning…

While reaching 1.0 is a big milestone, our work is far from done. We’re continuing to ship weekly updates with improvements, fixes, and new features, many of which are based directly on your feedback. Elements will keep evolving, and we’re committed to making it the best, most enjoyable way to build for the modern web. Stay tuned, there’s plenty more to come.

#### Your Feedback Matters!

By using Elements, [sharing your experiences](https://forums.realmacsoftware.com/c/rapidweaver-elements/43), and letting us know what works (and what could be better), you’re playing a huge role in shaping its future. We love hearing from you, so keep that feedback coming!

Thanks for being part of this journey, we can’t wait to see what you create with Elements!
