# Early Access



### 1.0 is just the beginning!

While reaching 1.0 is a big milestone, our work is far from done. We’re continuing to ship [weekly builds](https://forums.realmacsoftware.com/c/rapidweaver-elements/beta/59) with improvements, fixes, and new features, many of which are based directly on your feedback. Elements will keep evolving, and we’re committed to making it the best, most enjoyable way to build for the modern web. Stay tuned, there’s plenty more to come.

### Your Feedback Matters!

By using Elements, [sharing your experiences](https://forums.realmacsoftware.com/c/rapidweaver-elements/43), and letting us know what works (and what could be better), you’re playing a huge role in shaping its future. We love hearing from you, so keep that feedback coming!

Thanks for being part of this journey, we can’t wait to see what you create with Elements!
