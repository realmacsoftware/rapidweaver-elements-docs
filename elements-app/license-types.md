# License Types

With Elements’ three license options—Base, Plus, and Pro—users can choose the plan that best fits their needs, whether they’re casual users, hobbyists, or professional web designers. Each license is designed with different levels of functionality, but the Pro license stands out for those looking to use Elements in a commercial capacity and benefit from exclusive support.

All Element licenses include:

* 1 year of updates. Yours to keep forever.
* Allow installation on up to 2 Macs simultaneously.

### Why Choose a Pro License?

The Pro license allows users to develop and sell websites to third-party clients, making it the ideal choice for freelancers, agencies, or anyone building websites as part of their business. With this license, users have the freedom to fully leverage Elements’ powerful tools and customizations to create and deliver high-quality websites for clients, turning Elements into a valuable asset for generating revenue.
